## ⚡ Method 2: Native Figma Plugin (Creates Pure Figma Auto-Layout Frames)

If you prefer pure Figma Auto-Layout containers (`layoutMode: VERTICAL / HORIZONTAL`):

1. In Figma, click the **Figma Menu (F or Hamburger icon)** at the top-left.
2. Navigate to: **Plugins** → **Development** → **Import plugin from manifest...**
3. Browse and select:
   📁 `c:\Users\Jeho\Downloads\BayanihanHubAfter\figma-admin-generator\manifest.json`
4. Press `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`) in Figma, type **"BayanihanHub"**, and press **Enter** (or right-click canvas → **Plugins** → **Development** → **BayanihanHub Admin Wireframes Generator**).
5. The plugin runs in under 1 second and automatically builds all 6 artboards side-by-side on your canvas!
